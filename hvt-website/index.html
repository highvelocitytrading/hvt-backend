Signal Bars

"Green means buy. Red means sell. That's it."

HVT's proprietary indicator rows sit below your chart and update in real time. Each row synthesizes a different layer of market data — order flow, momentum, and structure — into a single color-coded output. When they align, conditions are right. When they conflict, you wait.

What you see on your chart
  • Multiple color-coded signal rows below every candle
  • Aligned colors = market is ready to trade
  • Conflicting or yellow = market is consolidating, stay flat
The result: You never have to interpret complex data yourself. The bars do it for you.
Configured Entries

"The software shows you exactly where to enter. You place the trade."

HVT synthesizes all active indicator data into a clear entry signal that appears directly on your chart as a labeled Buy or Sell marker. You see the level, you decide to take it, and you place the order manually. No automation — just complete clarity on where the setup is.

What you see on your chart
  • Buy or Sell markers plotted at the exact entry level
  • No signal = conditions aren't right, stay out
  • You execute manually — the software does the analysis
The result: No guessing where to enter. The software identifies it — you pull the trigger.
Order Flow Reading

"See the contracts moving through the market before price reacts."

Beneath every candle, HVT tracks live contract flow — how many contracts are trading per minute, where buy and sell limit orders are sitting, and whether real buying or selling pressure is driving the move. This is the information institutional traders use that most retail traders never see.

What you see on your chart
  • Live contract count — updated every minute
  • Buy LMT and Sell LMT order tracking below the chart
  • Real pressure beneath each candle, not just price
The result: Price is the last thing to react. You're reading what moves it first.
Built-In Risk Management

"The moment you enter, your stop and target are already set."

HVT is built around NinjaTrader's ATM (Advanced Trade Management) strategy. You place the order manually — but the instant it fills, your stop loss and profit target are automatically applied. You never have to set them yourself or move a stop mid-trade.

What you see on your chart
  • Stop loss and profit target applied automatically on fill
  • Pre-configured ATM strategy — no manual adjustments needed
  • Your risk is defined before you ever enter
The result: You place the trade. The ATM strategy handles everything after.
Multi-Timeframe Confirmation

"Your short-term entry is confirmed by the bigger picture — automatically."

HVT runs multiple timeframe signal rows simultaneously. The TF1 and TF signal indicators confirm that your short-term entry aligns with the broader trend and market structure. You're never trading a 1-minute setup against a dominant higher-timeframe move.

What you see on your chart
  • TF1 Signal and TF Signal indicator rows on your chart
  • Timeframe alignment visible in real time
  • Entries only fire when short and long-term signals agree
The result: Short-term precision. Long-term alignment. Every single trade.

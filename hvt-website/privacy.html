Section 01

Overview

At High Velocity Trading, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the steps we take to protect it. It applies to all information gathered through our website and related services.

High Velocity Trading does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has submitted information to us, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

Section 02

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you visit or interact with our website. We only collect information that you voluntarily provide to us. Some information, such as payment details, may be required in order to access certain products or services.

Full name
Email address
Phone number
Mailing address
IP address
Payment information

Payment information is collected only when you subscribe to or purchase our services, and is handled securely through our payment processor.

Section 03

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide and improve our services. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Provide our services and manage account access
  • Send emails, platform updates, and educational content
  • Improve your experience on our website
  • Process transactions and verify payment information
  • Respond to questions and support requests

You may unsubscribe from our email communications at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us directly.

Section 04

Sharing & Third Parties

In limited situations, we may share necessary information with third-party service providers — such as payment processors — only for the purpose of completing transactions securely. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any outside parties for marketing purposes.

Some areas of our platform may allow you to share information publicly, such as community features or chat rooms. Please use caution when sharing personal details in these spaces, as other users may see and use information you post. High Velocity Trading is not responsible for information you voluntarily disclose in public areas of the platform.

Section 05

Updating Your Information

If you need to update or correct your personal information, you can do so through your account dashboard or by contacting us directly. We want your information to be accurate and up to date at all times.

Section 06

Contact & Support

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to request access to your data, or need assistance with your account, please reach out to us directly. We are committed to addressing any concerns promptly and respectfully.

Email us at alerts@highvelocitytrading.com or call 786-461-4235 during business hours.

Section 07

Security

We use recognized industry practices to protect your personal information, including:

  • Secure server technology and encrypted connections (SSL/TLS)
  • Data encryption for sensitive information including payment details
  • Password-protected systems with restricted access controls

While no online system can guarantee complete security, we work diligently to safeguard your data and keep it private. In the event of a data breach that may affect your personal information, we will notify you in accordance with applicable law.

Section 08

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. If we make significant changes, we will notify you by email or through a notice on our website prior to the changes taking effect.

Your continued use of our services after any update constitutes your acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed.
